Capability observations

Availability ≠ sensor output
Squeeze, double-tap and haptics are not available as dedicated web APIs. You can mark the moment you attempt a gesture in your JSON session.

The browser provides Pointer Events and, on compatible devices, additional Touch Events; this is not a native Apple Pencil diagnostic. All measurements and files stay on your device. A constant twist: 0 does not establish that barrel-roll sensing works, nor that the Pencil lacks it. For the fullest coalesced data, use a recent iPadOS Safari version; some APIs require a secure context.
